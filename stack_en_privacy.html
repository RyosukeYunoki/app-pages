Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Neon Stack: Cyber Focus" (the "App") handles your information.

1. Data Collection

The App collects limited data solely for the purpose of providing the Global Leaderboard feature. The data collected includes:

  • Player Name: The nickname you enter to display on the ranking.
  • Game Score: Your high scores achieved in the game.
  • Device Identifier: An anonymous identifier is used to authenticate your device securely with our database without requiring personal registration.

2. Use of Data

The collected data (Name and Score) is stored in a secure cloud database (Google Firebase) and is publicly visible on the Global Leaderboard within the App. We do not use this data for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase to store ranking data and authenticate users anonymously. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for more information on how they handle data.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Since the authentication is anonymous and requires no personal details (like email or real name), it is safe for users of all ages.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
