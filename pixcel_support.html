Pixel Timer Support

Find answers to common questions below or contact us for further assistance.

How do I hide the buttons and clock?
Simply tap anywhere on the background (empty space). This toggles "Focus Mode," hiding all UI elements. Tap again to bring them back.
The sand isn't moving when I tilt my phone.
Please ensure you have granted "Motion & Fitness" permissions to the app. You can check this in your iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness.
How do I change the sand color?
Tap the Gear icon (Settings) at the bottom left. You can choose from various colors like Multi-color, Cyan, Pink, etc.
Does the timer work in the background?
Yes. The timer continues to run accurately even if you close the app or lock your screen. You will receive a notification when the time is up.
