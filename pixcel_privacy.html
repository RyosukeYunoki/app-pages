Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Pixel Timer ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Pixel Timer operates entirely locally on your device. We do not have servers, and we do not track your usage, location, or personal identity.

2. Device Permissions

The app requires access to your device's Motion Sensors (Gyroscope/Accelerometer).

  • This data is used solely in real-time to control the visual gravity of the sand particles within the app.
  • This data is processed locally and is never transmitted off your device.

3. Children’s Privacy

Our app does not collect any personal information from children. It is safe for users of all ages.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
