Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Pulse Sleep" (hereinafter referred to as "the App") handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information (such as names, addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses). We do not access your location data or contacts.

2. Data Usage

Any data settings configured within the App (such as breathing pattern selection, theme colors, and haptic strength) are stored locally on your device solely to provide the App's functionality. This data is never sent to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not include third-party advertising services or user analytics tools (such as Google Analytics).

4. Apple HealthKit

If future updates include integration with Apple HealthKit, we will revise this policy and obtain your explicit permission before accessing any health data. The current version does not integrate with HealthKit.
